The service

ClipSource Cloud syncs your ClipSource clipboard history between your devices. The Mac app is open source under the MIT license; these terms cover the hosted sync service only.

Your account and passphrase

You are responsible for your sign-in account and your sync passphrase. Because clips are end-to-end encrypted, a lost passphrase cannot be recovered and the synced data cannot be decrypted.

Plans and billing

The Free plan includes sync for 2 Macs, the web app, and 7 days of synced history. Pro includes unlimited Macs and 365 days of synced history, billed monthly or yearly through Stripe. You can cancel at any time from the account page; Pro stays active until the end of the paid period. [REFUND POLICY AND TAXES TO BE SET]
