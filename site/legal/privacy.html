Summary

The ClipSource Mac app keeps your clipboard history on your Mac. Nothing leaves your Mac unless you turn on sync. When you do, your clips are end-to-end encrypted on your devices before upload, and we cannot read them.

What we store when you use ClipSource Cloud

  • Account: the email address and profile ID from the GitHub or Google sign-in you choose.
  • Encrypted clips: ciphertext, a keyed hash used to avoid duplicates, timestamps, and a random ID for the device that wrote each row. The text, app, window title and label of each clip are inside the ciphertext.
  • Passphrase check: a random salt and an encrypted known value, so your devices can verify your passphrase. Your passphrase is never sent.
  • Devices: the name and type (Mac or web) of each device you connect, and when it was last seen. Device names are not end-to-end encrypted.
  • Billing: your plan, subscription status and Stripe customer ID. Card details are handled by Stripe and never reach us.
