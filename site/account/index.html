    Removing a device signs it out immediately. If a device was lost or stolen, also delete your synced data below and set a new sync passphrase on your Mac, since the lost device may still know the old one.

    Delete my synced data

    Removes every synced clip, your sync passphrase check and your device list from the server. Local history on your Macs is not touched. Your plan and billing stay as they are.
