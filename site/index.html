Know where every copy came from — on every device

ClipSource is a menu-bar clipboard manager for macOS. Every copy is tagged with the app, the window or chat, and the AI session that made it. Turn on ClipSource Cloud and the same history follows you to your other Macs and the web, end-to-end encrypted.

Features

The clipboard, with a memory for sources

Three Claude Code sessions, a WhatsApp group, Slack and a browser all write to one clipboard. ClipSource tells them apart.

Source on every entry

App, focused window or chat title, label, session ID and project folder, recorded at the moment of the copy.

⌥⌘V search panel

Search the whole history, browse recent copies or group them by app. Copy again with Return or ⌘1–9.

CLI and MCP for AI agents

clipsrc copy --label "Sam reply" replaces pbcopy, and the MCP server lets agents copy with a label and search history.

Sync across devices

Sign in with GitHub or Google. Your Macs share one history, and any phone or PC can read and send clips on the web.

End-to-end encrypted

Clips are encrypted on your device with a key from your passphrase. The server stores ciphertext it cannot read.

Private by default

History stays in local SQLite until you turn sync on. Password-manager copies and excluded apps are never stored.

How end-to-end encryption works

Your passphrase never leaves your devices

  1. Choose a sync passphrase on your Mac

    A key is derived from it on the device (PBKDF2-SHA256, 600,000 rounds). The passphrase itself is never stored or sent.

  2. Each clip is sealed before upload

    Text and source details are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Duplicates are matched by a keyed HMAC, not by content.

  3. Other devices unlock with the same passphrase

    Your other Macs and the web app derive the same key locally and decrypt in place.

What the server can see

  • Encrypted blobs, keyed hashes and timestamps
  • A random ID for the device that wrote each row
  • Your device list (names and type), shown on your account page
  • Your plan and billing status

FAQ

Questions

Do I need an account to use ClipSource?

No. The Mac app, local history, widgets, CLI and MCP server work without an account. You only sign in to turn on sync.

Can ClipSource read my clips?

No. Clips are encrypted on your devices with a key derived from your sync passphrase. The server stores only ciphertext, keyed hashes and timestamps.

What happens if I forget my sync passphrase?

Nobody can recover it, including us. Delete your synced data from the account page and set a new passphrase; your local history on each Mac is untouched.

Does the web app count as a device?

No. The Free plan includes 2 Macs, and the web app works on any number of browsers on top of that.

What happens to my history if I downgrade?

Your devices stay connected, synced history older than 7 days is removed, and you cannot add a third Mac until you remove one. Local history on each Mac is not affected.

Can I run my own server?

Yes. ClipSource is open source and the sync backend is a Supabase project. In the Mac app choose Settings → Sync → Advanced to use your own server; the web app asks for it when no server is configured.

Is the Chrome extension included?

Yes. The Chrome extension is free and works locally with the Mac app; it is not part of any plan.
